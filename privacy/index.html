Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-22. This page explains what MoviesParty collects and why.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Information we handle

  • Room data: room codes, display names and playback state needed to run a shared session.
  • Chat, voice and video: transmitted between participants during a session so the feature works.
  • Local storage: your browser may store preferences on your device. This stays on your device.

Third-party services

  • TMDB: movie posters load from image.tmdb.org, so TMDB receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Google Fonts: fonts load from Google servers, which receive your IP address and browser details.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data. Write to contact@moviesparty.com.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.

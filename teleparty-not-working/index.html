Teleparty not working: fixes to try first

Teleparty is a browser extension, so most problems come from the browser, another extension or the streaming site. Work through these checks in order.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Quick answer: Update the extension and browser, reload the page, disable other extensions, make sure everyone is on the same streaming account type and title, and try again in a clean browser profile.

Check the basics

  1. Reload and rejoin. Everyone should refresh the streaming page and open the party link again.
  2. Update the extension and the browser. An out-of-date extension can break when a streaming site changes.
  3. Check that the extension is switched on in your browser’s extension menu and allowed to run on the streaming site.
  4. Everyone needs access to the same title. A movie may be available in one country or on one plan and not another.
  5. Be signed in to the same streaming service, with a profile that can play the title.

Other extensions and settings

  • Turn off ad blockers, VPNs and other extensions one by one to see whether one of them interferes.
  • Try a fresh browser profile or a private window with only Teleparty enabled.
  • Clear the cache and cookies for the streaming site.
  • Check that hardware acceleration and your browser version are supported by the streaming site.

Out of sync or buffering

  • Use a stable connection and close big downloads.
  • Pause, make sure everyone is at the same timestamp and press play again.
  • Lower the video quality one step on the streaming site.

Details about Teleparty’s features and supported sites change. For anything that is specific to it, check the extension’s own listing and support page.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Teleparty not syncing?

The common causes are an out-of-date extension, another extension interfering, different movie versions or a poor connection. Reload, update and test with other extensions turned off.

Do I need the same subscription as my friends?

Each person needs access to the same title on the streaming service. Availability can differ by country or plan.

Is there a watch party option that does not use an extension?

Yes. MoviesParty is a Windows app with room codes, so there is nothing to install in the browser. Everyone in the room needs the app.

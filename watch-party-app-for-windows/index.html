A watch party app for Windows

A desktop app keeps the room, the countdown and the chat in one window. Here is what the MoviesParty Windows app does and how to get your friends in.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Quick answer: MoviesParty is a free watch party app for Windows. Install it, create a room, send the code, and everyone presses play together with voice, video and chat beside the player.

What the app does

  • Room codes. You create a room and share a short code. Friends enter it in the app and join.
  • Synced playback. The host starts a countdown and everyone begins on the same frame. Pause and seek stay in sync.
  • Voice, video and chat. They sit beside the player, so you do not need a separate call.
  • Movies in the app. Pick a movie in the room and everyone watches it together, right there in the app.

Get started in four steps

  1. Download the Windows app from the MoviesParty home page and install it.
  2. Create a room and pick whether it is private. Private rooms come with Premium.
  3. Send the room code to your friends. They install the app and enter the code.
  4. When everyone is in, the host starts the countdown and you press play together.

Free and Premium

The free plan includes 1080p, public rooms and chat. Premium adds 4K Ultra HD, private rooms and priority sync. Pricing for Premium has not been announced.

What about Mac, phone and browser?

The Windows app is available today and macOS is on the way. Rooms run in the app, not in the browser, and everyone in the room needs the app. If your group uses different devices, check that each person can install it before you plan the night.

Tips for a smooth night

  • Have everyone install the app and test their microphone before movie night.
  • Use headphones to stop the film from echoing into voice chat.

If something goes wrong, the troubleshooting guide covers echo, microphone and sync problems.

How to watch movies together online

You do not need to be on the same couch to share a movie night. Here are the main ways to watch movies together online, what to check before you pick one, and how to host a session with MoviesParty.

Three ways to watch movies together online

1. Watch parties built into streaming services

Some streaming services include their own group viewing, for example Disney+ GroupWatch, Hulu Watch Party and Amazon Prime Video Watch Party. They are convenient, but each one only covers that service’s catalog and usually needs everyone to have an account with it. Features and limits change, so check the service’s help page before you plan a night.

2. Browser extensions

Extensions such as Teleparty (formerly Netflix Party) keep play, pause and seeking in sync on supported streaming websites and add a group chat. Everyone installs the extension and, in most cases, needs their own subscription to the streaming service.

3. Watch party websites and apps

Watch party sites like MoviesParty are built around the shared session itself. You create a room, send a code and press play together, with voice, video and text chat beside the player. MoviesParty does not host movies: each person plays their own copy or their own subscription, and the site syncs the playhead so everyone sees the same frame in the same second.

What to check before you choose a tool

  • Subscriptions: does everyone need a paid account, and with which service?
  • Sync: does the tool put everyone back on the same frame after a pause or a seek?
  • Talking: can you use voice, video and text chat in the same window as the player?
  • Devices: does it work in the browsers and on the phones your friends use?
  • Price: is the basic version free, and what do paid plans add?

Host a movie night with MoviesParty in three steps

  1. Create a room. Pick a name and press the button. Your room is ready in a second.
  2. Send the code. Share the room code with your friends. They type it on the site and walk right in.
  3. Press play together. When everyone is in, the host starts the countdown and everyone begins on the same frame.

Tips for a smoother movie night

  • Make sure everyone is watching the same version of the film. Different cuts or releases can drift out of sync.
  • Use a stable connection and close other apps that use a lot of bandwidth.
  • Agree on a start time and have everyone join a few minutes early.
  • Wear headphones during voice chat so the movie audio does not echo into the call.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watch movies together online for free?

Yes. MoviesParty’s free plan includes 1080p, public rooms and chat, and many other watch party tools have free tiers too.

Does everyone need the same streaming subscription?

It depends on the tool. Built-in watch parties and browser extensions usually require each person to have their own account. With MoviesParty, each person plays their own copy or their own subscription and the site only syncs the playback position.

Do we need to install anything?

MoviesParty works from the browser, phone included. Windows and macOS apps are on the way.

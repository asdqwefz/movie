A WatchParty.me alternative for movie nights

WatchParty.me is a website for watching videos together with screen sharing, a shared virtual browser and chat. Here is how MoviesParty differs for full movie nights.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Quick answer: WatchParty.me works in the browser and offers several ways to share video, including screen sharing and a cloud browser. MoviesParty is a Windows app where you pick a movie in the room and everyone watches it together in the app.

How the two differ

When WatchParty.me is the better choice

  • You want to share a browser tab or a whole cloud browser with a group, not only a movie.
  • You need a room that starts instantly in the browser with nothing to install.
  • You like the idea of an open-source tool you can self-host.

When MoviesParty is the better choice

  • You want a dedicated app for movie nights instead of a shared browser.
  • You want each person to see the film at their own quality instead of watching someone else’s shared screen. In our watch party vs screen sharing guide we explain why.
  • You want a room code and a shared countdown in one app, with voice, video and chat already built in.

Things to know before you switch

  • MoviesParty is a Windows app today, and everyone in the room needs it. macOS is on the way.
  • MoviesParty does not use screen sharing, so picture quality does not depend on one person’s upload speed.

Frequently asked questions

Is MoviesParty a WatchParty.me alternative?

Yes, for movie nights where you want synced playback with voice, video and chat in one app. If you want screen sharing or a shared cloud browser, WatchParty.me covers that.

Does MoviesParty use screen sharing?

No. MoviesParty does not use screen sharing, so picture quality does not depend on one person’s upload speed.

Is MoviesParty free?

Yes. The free plan includes 1080p, public rooms and chat. Premium adds 4K Ultra HD, private rooms and priority sync; pricing has not been announced yet.
